Jeewon Kim

I am a Robotics Researcher and a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical Engineering at KAIST (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology), where I also received my B.S. degree.

My research focuses on 3D Scene Graphs, Robust Robot Autonomy (Localization & Mapping), and intelligent robotic systems utilizing Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models.
